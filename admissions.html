A practical first step

How to enquire

  1. 01

    Contact or visit the school

    Speak with the school administration in Kakuma to ask about current admission availability and the right contact person.

  2. 02

    Confirm grade and curriculum

    Ask which grade placements are available and which curriculum pathway applies to the learner.

  3. 03

    Request the current requirements

    Get the official application steps, document list, fee information, and submission dates directly from the school.

  4. 04

    Follow the school's application process

    Submit information only through the contact or process the school confirms, then ask how and when a decision will be communicated.
